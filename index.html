Ask Kowe Why the beta-lactam ring opens

Why does the beta-lactam ring break so easily? I keep mixing up the strain with the leaving group.

The ring has only four atoms, so the bonds are bent away from the shape they want. That stored strain is why a nucleophile can open it.

The leaving group matters later, when the bond has already broken. Strain explains why it opens. The leaving group explains what leaves afterwards.

opens into
  1. A nucleophile moves toward the carbonyl carbon on the ring.
  2. The four-membered ring springs open and the strain drops.
  3. The leaving group departs. This is a separate step from the strain.
0:42

“The ring is already pulled tight. A small push, and it springs open. The leaving group is what happens next, not the reason it breaks.”
